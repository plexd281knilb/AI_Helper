Google Calendar Integration & Permissions

AI Helper integrates with Google Calendar via secure OAuth 2.0 authentication to streamline family and personal schedules. Here is how our integration operates in full compliance with the Google API Services User Data Policy:

⚡

Automated Synchronization

Pushes confirmed events directly to your primary Google Calendar with verified start times, durations, room/campus locations, and full context summaries.

🛡️

Duplicate Prevention Engine

Inspects existing Google Calendar entries in a +/- 14-hour window before creating any event to ensure identical school activities or meetings are not duplicated.

🔒

Limited Use Compliance

Adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy Limited Use requirements. Calendar access is used strictly to sync user-requested events.

🚫

Zero Third-Party Sharing

Your Google user data is NEVER sold, shared with advertisers, or used for training AI models. All tokens remain stored locally on your private server.

Comprehensive Assistant Capabilities

Designed from the ground up for modern family scheduling and everyday productivity.

📬 Inbox Safety & IMAP Peek

Analyzes emails using non-destructive BODY.PEEK per RFC 3501, keeping your incoming messages unread and untouched in your inbox.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Member Attribution

Intelligently matches extracted events to specific family members based on grade level (2nd, 6th, 9th) and sports context (Cross Country, Softball, Track).

🛒 Local Deals & Smart Groceries

Aggregates weekly circulars from local supermarkets (H-E-B, Tom Thumb, Costco) to compare prices and plan cohesive family dinners.

⚡ 1-Click Calendar Controls

Instant manual sync, bulk push, or 1-click dismissal with automatic calendar deletion so your schedule remains synchronized and accurate.
